# Anthropic IPO

A reading of Anthropic’s preliminary S-1, filed with the SEC on October 26, 2026.

The price is blank. The share count is blank. These pages keep the numbers that are already in the filing, and they leave the blanks blank.

!!! warning "Preliminary"
    The registration statement is not effective. Figures in this draft can change. This site is a reading of a public filing. It is not an offer and not advice.

## What is here

The pictures are the charts. Each one is a single question from the prospectus: revenue, where a dollar went, the loss, the compute promises, the customers, the share classes, and the companies that both fund Anthropic and compete with it.

The filing pages are the cover of the document: what was filed, what is being sold, and which lines are still empty.
