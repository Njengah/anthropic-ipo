Net revenue retention of about 900% is as of September 30, 2026. It is current-quarter revenue divided by the quarter a year earlier, for customers with at least $1 in that base quarter. Claude Pro and Max are left out.

Adjusted operating margin in the second quarter of 2026 was 19%. GAAP operating margin was 8%. The difference in that quarter is the $1,261 million of stock-based pay added back. Adjusted figures do not add back the fair-value marks.

Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments at June 30, 2026 were $131.7 billion. Cash and equivalents were $112.4 billion. Short-term investments were $19.3 billion. Capital raised in aggregate, as of that date, was $141.1 billion.

If the offering had closed on June 30, 2026, the company would have recorded $2.9 billion of stock-based compensation that day for RSUs whose service condition was already met. No expense had been recognized for those RSUs, because the registration statement was not yet effective.

Government contracts were about 1% of revenue in the periods discussed.

Cloud marketplaces were 11% of revenue in 2023, 32% in 2024, 47% in 2025, and 48% in the first half of 2026. At June 30, 2026, marketplace sales were 58% of net accounts receivable.
