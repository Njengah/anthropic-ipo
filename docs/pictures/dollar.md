| Operating profit | $974 million | 8% |

Stock-based pay inside personnel that quarter was $1,261 million. Adjusted operating profit, which adds that pay back, was $2,235 million, a 19% margin. GAAP operating margin was 8%.

In the first quarter of 2025, compute was 251% of revenue. That is a different quarter from the second quarter of 2025, when compute was 177% of revenue.
