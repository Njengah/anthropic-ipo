# The loss

Second quarter of 2026. Operating profit was $974 million. Net loss was $66,309 million.

The bridge, in millions: operating profit 974, plus interest income 762, minus a fair-value mark of 59,597, minus other expense of 8,448, equals a net loss of 66,309.

The fair-value mark is the private valuation of convertible notes, derivatives, and SAFEs moving up, including the Series H round in that quarter. It is an accounting mark. Operating cash flow for the first half of 2026 was positive $3,929 million.

The other expense is mainly the fair value of assets and liabilities under the note-purchase agreements.

About $113.2 billion of the accumulated deficit since 2023 is these fair-value changes, including $72.4 billion in the first half of 2026. The accumulated deficit at June 30, 2026 was $132,902 million.

The third quarter of 2025 operating loss includes the $1.5 billion Bartz settlement.
