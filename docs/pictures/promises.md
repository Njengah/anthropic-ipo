# Promises

The compute agreements are different legal shapes. The prospectus does not present them as one check. The rolled-up total in the summary is blank.

<iframe title="Compute commitments, kept separate" src="../../visuals/04-promises.html" style="width:100%;height:860px;border:1px solid #e4ddd3;border-radius:12px;background:#f7f4ee;"></iframe>

Firm minimums or non-cancelable leases:

- Google: at least about $114.5 billion, April 2026 through July 2033.
- Amazon: at least $110.0 billion, May 2026 through April 2036.
- Microsoft: at least $44.9 billion, November 2026 through May 2033.
- Broadcom: equipment-lease rent of about $161.2 billion over the life of the leases, excluding interest.

Cancellable:

- SpaceXAI: up to $120.5 billion. After non-cancelable periods ending January 2027 and March 2027, either side can cancel on 90 days’ notice.

Estimates, or fees only if capacity is delivered:

- Additional Google TPU capacity for 2028–2031: Anthropic estimates $125 billion to $150 billion. This estimate is not in the obligations table.
- Nscale: up to about $44.6 billion, fees as capacity is accepted.
- Lambda: about $41.3 billion, fees as capacity is accepted. About $34.9 billion of that is the August 2026 agreement.
- AMD: no fixed dollar commitment. If Anthropic takes the full initial Helios tranche in 2027, it expects to spend more than $20 billion.

Customer revenue is mostly consumption and can be reduced on short notice. The compute side is multi-year.
