# Builders

What the filing says about how fast the models turn over, and about Anthropic’s own use of Claude.

Models released in the six months ended September 30, 2026 were over 80% of revenue in the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The dollar amount of that quarter is blank.

Opus 5.5 reduced customer cost per task by about 40% versus Opus 5. The company says usage rose and margins held.

Employees: about 940 at December 31, 2024, and about 5,300 at September 30, 2026. Trailing-twelve-month revenue per employee was over $6.6 million at September 30, 2026, against $1.4 million a year earlier.

In September 2026, the company says over 90% of contributions to its own codebase were written by Claude. The prospectus does not define “contribution.” Average daily code volume per engineer in the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was over 45 times the 2024 daily average.

Claude Code’s research preview was February 2025. Claude Cowork’s was January 2026. As of September 30, 2026, Cowork’s adoption had outpaced Claude Code’s adoption at the equivalent point after that February preview.

In the September quarter, over 50% of the $100,000 customers used the Claude Platform and at least one first-party application, up from about 30% in the December 2025 quarter.
