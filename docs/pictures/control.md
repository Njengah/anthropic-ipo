# The share

Five classes after the offering. Class A is the share in this IPO. It has the economics and one vote. It does not elect the board majority.

| Class | Economics | Vote |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Class A | Yes. Dividends and liquidation, pro rata. | One vote per share. Elects the directors who are not Class T directors. |
| Class N | Same economics as Class A. | Generally none. Converts into Class A if sold to an unaffiliated outsider. |
| Class F | Par value. One share, issued to Founder LLC. | 50.1% of the general vote, including election of the non-Class T directors. |
| Class T | Par value. Held by the Long-Term Benefit Trust. | Elects the Class T directors, a majority of the authorized board. No vote on ordinary business. |
| Class E | Par value. For employees other than the co-founders. | A narrow list of matters. Outstanding after the offering: 0 shares in the offering table. |

The seven co-founders are the initial members of Founder LLC. Together, the trust and Founder LLC can elect every director.

Anthropic will be a controlled company under Nasdaq rules because the trust can elect a majority of the authorized board. It says it expects to use that exemption for the nominating committee. The audit and compensation committees are entirely independent. The board currently has a majority of independent directors.

The Anthropic Public Benefit Fund holds 846,840,960 shares of Class N, described as 5% of fully diluted shares at June 30, 2026, assuming the Amazon notes convert. The board may later issue up to an extra 2% at each 50% price milestone. A milestone counts only if the close has cleared it on at least 90 of the trailing 120 trading days. The issuance is discretionary.

Each co-founder has pledged 80% of their personal equity to philanthropic causes. The filing says the pledges are voluntary and revocable. They do not cover the Class F share.

Lock-up: co-founders and holders above 0.5% fully diluted, until the earlier of the trading day after the September 30, 2027 earnings release and the 365th day after the prospectus. Other holders in the agreements, until the earlier of the day after the March 31, 2027 earnings release and the 180th day.
