# Customers

The prospectus does not keep an internal segment called enterprise. It uses customers with at least $100,000 of trailing-twelve-month revenue as that measure.

Counts of customers at $100,000 or more: about 20 at the end of 2023, about 200 at the end of 2024, about 1,500 at the end of 2025, more than 6,000 at June 30, 2026, and more than 11,900 at September 30, 2026.

As of September 30, 2026: more than 1,900 customers at $1 million or more, and about 250 at $10 million or more. About 40 million customers were under $100,000. The revenue share of that long tail, at that date, is blank.

The 74% figure is a different date. In the first half of 2026, the $100,000-and-up group produced $12.0 billion, about 74% of that half. At June 30, 2026 the group was still more than 6,000 customers.

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, no customer was more than 10% of revenue. The top five were about 25%.

Net revenue retention was about 900% as of September 30, 2026. A qualified customer had at least $1 of revenue in the quarter a year earlier. Claude Pro and Max are excluded, because those plans are a fixed price.

About 5% to 7% of revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 came from customers later judged to be breaking the usage rules. Enforcement stepped up in August and September 2026.
