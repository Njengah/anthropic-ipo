# Competition

The filing names two primary competitors: Google and OpenAI.

It also says Amazon, Google, and Microsoft are each an investor, a customer, a distributor, and a competitor. Each develops and deploys its own models on the cloud that sells Claude.

The three clouds can see Anthropic’s pricing and commercial terms, and they can see its operations and compute requirements.

A further category is open-weight and open-source models from state actors and from research organizations. The filing says they compete where buyers want a cheaper model, and that this can put pricing pressure on Claude. No individual lab is named in that passage.

The market section also treats the status quo as competition: legacy systems, retraining, and buyers who would rather not change.
