# The offering

Class A common stock. Anthropic has applied to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT. There is no public market before this offering.

The company is selling shares. Selling stockholders are selling more shares. Anthropic receives nothing from the selling stockholders’ shares. The underwriters have an option to buy additional shares from the company. Every share count on the cover is blank.

The price range is blank. The underwriting discount is blank. Proceeds to the company and proceeds to the selling stockholders are blank. Delivery is in New York, on a date the cover leaves blank.

Lead underwriters: Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley. The syndicate also includes J.P. Morgan, Citigroup, Barclays, Wells Fargo Securities, BofA Securities, Deutsche Bank Securities, RBC Capital Markets, UBS Investment Bank, BMO Capital Markets, BNP Paribas, Credit Agricole CIB, Jefferies, Mizuho, MUFG, Societe Generale, TD Securities, BTIG, Cantor, Capital One Securities, and Macquarie Capital.

The offering also includes public offerings in Australia, Canada, certain member states of the European Economic Area, Japan, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom. Anthropic does not plan to list on an exchange in those places.

On September 17, 2026 the company split Class A and Class N 10-for-1. Share counts in the prospectus are after that split. The same day, Class T was reverse-split 1-for-1,000.

Stated uses of the money: working capital, operating expenses, capital spending including compute, and hiring. A portion is for tax withholding when certain RSUs settle. That dollar amount is blank. The company may also buy other businesses. It says it has no agreement for a material acquisition.

No cash dividend has been paid. The credit agreement limits dividends even when nothing is drawn.
